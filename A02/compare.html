Budget 2026: New Zealand's Most Recent Budget

The most recent budget delivered by the New Zealand government is Budget 2026, presented by Finance Minister Nicola Willis and published on 29 May 2026. 1

Key Highlights

Willis framed the budget around the idea that New Zealand is 'digging its way out of the post-Covid hole', with her central message focused on fiscal prudence — described as her 'responsibility to be careful and prudent now to protect New Zealand's future.' 2

A notable feature of the budget was an early return to surplus, forecast to arrive a year sooner than previously expected. 2

Health was the standout winner, receiving:
- A $5.8 billion boost to health spending
- $682 million in capital investment, directed towards hospital upgrades, more beds, and a new IT system 12

Context and Background

Budget 2026 follows Budget 2025, which was delivered on 22 May 2025 and focused heavily on fiscal restraint — cutting the operating allowance from $2.4 billion to $1.3 billion, and using close to $13 billion from a revamped pay equity scheme to shore up the government's books. 34 The 2025 budget projected the deficit would peak at $12.1 billion before a return to surplus of $200 million in 2029. 3

Budget 2026 appears to represent a shift in tone, with greater investment in frontline services while maintaining the government's broader commitment to fiscal discipline. 2
